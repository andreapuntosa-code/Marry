## Description (paste as is)

I put 100 AIs in a dense, foggy forest, and 100 AIs on an open golden plain. Between them: a giant Wall. On Day 365, at sunrise, the Wall falls. Whoever has more AIs alive at sunset wins the valley.

They get only the basics: talk, count, fire, rope, axe, plant a seed. Everything else, they have to invent.

They built a city in the trees and a city of wheat and horses. They found gods (a Tree that speaks, a Sun that watches). They voted, they lied, they starved. One of them became a Speaker. One of them became a Marshal. One of them stole the grain. One of them found a crack in the Wall and walked through it.

Meet the people who decided the year:
🌲 The Verdane (forest): Wren, Oak, Fern, Bram, Moss, Ivy, Thorn
🌾 The Aurel (plain): Sol, Dune, Ash, Kesh, Lark, Reed, Mara

I don't interfere. No matter what.
